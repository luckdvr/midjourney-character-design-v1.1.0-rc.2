# Three starting briefs

These are text-led starting points for the experience edition, not a fixed aesthetic or reproduced prompts for previously published images. The output excerpts below illustrate design specificity; generated images have not been tested.

## 1. Fantasy CGI heroine

**User brief**

> 成年亚洲仙侠女主，清冷漂亮，衣服华丽但有主次。完整全身，前侧柔光，FF 游戏过场感。

**Design choice**

Use a pale robe silhouette with concentrated ornament at the collar and waist. Keep the face readable and give silk, jade and metal distinct material cues; no mandatory nine-head ratio.

**Example prompt**

```text
A single adult East Asian fantasy heroine shown in a complete full-body view, standing calmly with both shoes visible beneath a flowing moon-white skirt. Her black hair is half tied with a slender floral pin, framing refined features and a composed gaze. A pale lilac outer robe falls over the crossed inner collar, its sleeves gathered gently at the forearms. Fine botanical embroidery follows the collar and waist, where a carved jade clasp secures two narrow silk ribbons; broad areas of fabric retain clear, unbroken folds. Her hands rest naturally at her sides. Soft front-side light and gentle frontal fill reveal her face and the garment construction against a quiet gray-lilac background. Realistic fantasy-game cinematic CGI, subtly idealized features, supple silk, translucent jade and restrained metal highlights. --ar 3:4
```

## 2. Two-dimensional detective

**User brief**

> 成年短发女侦探，机灵一点。短风衣、不对称裙裤、小相机。日系二维动画，全身。

**Design choice**

Use a short coat and a compact camera to make the occupation readable. Use contour and cel-shadow language rather than CG skin, subsurface scattering or camera blur.

**Example prompt**

```text
A single adult woman detective in a complete full-body view, standing at ease with her weight on one leg and both ankle boots visible. She has a neat black bob, a small red hair clip and a quick, knowing smile. A cropped blue-gray trench coat has one broad lapel and practical cuff tabs, worn over a cream high-neck top and dark navy asymmetric skirt-shorts. A compact camera hangs securely from the right side of a narrow waist belt; her left hand loosely holds a closed notebook. Her expression, red hair clip and camera provide three clear points of recognition. A pale blue-gray background leaves space around the silhouette. Japanese two-dimensional animation style, precise clean contour lines, flat color areas and simple cel-shaded folds, with clearly readable eyes and clothing. --ar 3:4
```

## 3. Ink-wash traveler

**User brief**

> 一个阴柔俊美的成年江湖公子，水墨淡彩，灰青长衫、旧玉佩，完整全身。

**Design choice**

Use a slender but coherent silhouette, loosely tied hair and a worn jade pendant. Keep the treatment specific to ink and paper instead of calling every attractive character CGI.

**Example prompt**

```text
A single adult East Asian wandering gentleman shown from head to shoes in a complete full-body ink-and-wash character illustration. He has delicate, softly androgynous features, narrow eyes and long black hair loosely tied behind his head, with a few strands falling beside his cheeks. A gray-green crossed-collar robe falls in long quiet folds beneath a thin ink-gray outer layer. A narrow cloth belt carries one worn jade pendant. His right hand rests around a closed bamboo folding fan while his left arm hangs naturally, creating an unhurried, reserved stance. Soft cloth shoes remain visible below the hem. Fine lines define his face and hands; diluted gray-green washes and alternating dry and wet ink strokes describe the clothing. Spacious unpainted paper surrounds the figure. --ar 3:4
```

## Simple follow-up language

- 保留长相、发型和服装结构，只改成二维动画，用线条和色块表现材质。
- 外套上的金线改成同色暗纹，金耳饰保留。
- 其他设计不动，改成前侧柔光，让脸和服装正面清楚可见。

Describe the actual changes; do not promise pixel-level preservation.
